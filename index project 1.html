Favorite Foods

Favorite Music

And lastly we'll end on music. I really like the rock genre, and I rarely leave the 60s-80s era of music when listening.

1. "Kodachrome" – Paul Simon

Genre: Folk Rock

Apparently this song is about nostalgia, growing up, and how music and photography make memories feel brighter than real life.

Listen & Info: Paul Simon Official Site

2. "Latitude 88 North" – Electric Light Orchestra (ELO/Jeff Lynne)

Genre: Progressive Rock

I really like this song for its intricacy and atmospheric sound. Apparently, this song is about lonlieness, but that's not why I like it.

Listen & Info: ELO Official Site

3. "Movin' Out (Anthony's Song)" – Billy Joel

Genre: Pop Rock

This song follows a story that Billy Joel sometimes likes to encorporate into his music. It's a critique of the persuit of the American Dream.

Listen & Info: Billy Joel Official Site
